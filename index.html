What this is

Ontological Praxis is a body of work about how attention becomes structure, how perception becomes form, and how private obsessions become artifacts another mind can use.

The work begins with small signals: a water stain on asphalt, a phrase that will not leave, an ant trail, a sound experiment, a disliked herb, a screenshot saved for no clear reason, a field note from an ordinary place.

The point is not to make small things precious. The point is to notice when small things open into structure.

Field notes / artifacts

Current lines of inquiry include ordinary spaces as field stations, incompatible perceptual worlds, surface traces, insect-scale signal systems, contact microphones, weather traces, and the instability of the obvious.

The recurring question is simple: what becomes visible when attention treats the ordinary world as evidence?
